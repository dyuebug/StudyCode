# Day 1 英文自我介绍 - 写作指导与范文

---

## 🎯 任务说明

**场景**: 你在一个国际学术会议上遇到一位外国教授，需要用英文自我介绍。

**要求**:
- 5-8句话
- 包含：姓名、专业、考研准备、研究兴趣、学英语的原因

**评分标准**:
- ✅ 内容完整性 (5分)
- ✅ 语法准确性 (3分)
- ✅ 表达地道性 (2分)

---

## 📝 范文示例

### 版本1: 简洁版 (适合初学者)

> Hi, my name is [Zhang Wei]. I'm a senior majoring in Computer Science at [XX University]. I'm currently preparing for the postgraduate entrance examination, hoping to pursue a master's degree in Computer Science. My research interest lies in artificial intelligence, particularly in machine learning and natural language processing. I'm learning English because I want to read academic papers, communicate with international researchers, and potentially study abroad in the future. It's a pleasure to meet you!

**中文对照**:
你好，我叫[张伟]。我是[XX大学]计算机科学专业的大四学生。我目前正在准备研究生入学考试，希望攻读计算机科学硕士学位。我的研究兴趣在于人工智能，特别是机器学习和自然语言处理。我学习英语是因为我想阅读学术论文，与国际研究人员交流，并可能在未来出国留学。很高兴见到您！

---

### 版本2: 详细版 (适合进阶)

> Good morning, Professor. My name is [Li Hua], and I'm a final-year undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at [Tsinghua University]. I'm currently preparing for the national postgraduate entrance examination, with the goal of pursuing a master's degree in artificial intelligence. My primary research interests include deep learning, computer vision, and their applications in real-world problems. I've been actively improving my English because I believe it's essential for reading cutting-edge research papers, collaborating with international teams, and staying updated with the latest developments in the field. I'm particularly excited about the possibility of contributing to open-source projects and engaging with the global tech community. It's truly an honor to meet you here!

**中文对照**:
早上好，教授。我叫[李华]，是[清华大学]计算机科学专业的应届本科生。我目前正在准备全国研究生入学考试，目标是攻读人工智能硕士学位。我的主要研究兴趣包括深度学习、计算机视觉及其在现实问题中的应用。我一直在积极提高我的英语水平，因为我相信这对于阅读前沿研究论文、与国际团队合作以及了解该领域的最新发展至关重要。我对为开源项目做出贡献和参与全球技术社区的可能性感到特别兴奋。很荣幸在这里见到您！

---

### 版本3: 个性化版 (突出个人特色)

> Hello, I'm [Wang Ming], a computer science student at [Fudan University]. I'm currently in my final year and preparing for the postgraduate entrance exam. What really drives me is the intersection of systems programming and distributed computing – I find it fascinating how we can make computers work together efficiently. Besides preparing for the exam, I'm also working on a personal project involving container orchestration. I'm learning English not just for academic purposes, but also because most of the best technical documentation and community discussions happen in English. Reading Linus Torvalds' emails and Rust RFCs has been both challenging and incredibly rewarding. I'd love to hear about your research!

**中文对照**:
你好，我是[王明]，[复旦大学]的计算机科学专业学生。我目前在大四，正在准备研究生入学考试。真正驱动我的是系统编程和分布式计算的交叉领域——我发现如何让计算机高效协同工作非常迷人。除了准备考试，我还在做一个关于容器编排的个人项目。我学英语不仅仅是为了学术目的，也因为大多数最好的技术文档和社区讨论都是用英语进行的。阅读Linus Torvalds的邮件和Rust RFC既具有挑战性又非常有益。我很想听听您的研究！

## 📖 句型模板与替换

### 1. 自我介绍开头

**基础模板**:
```
Hi/Hello/Good morning, my name is [Name].
```

**进阶表达**:
```
Allow me to introduce myself. I'm [Name].
It's a pleasure to meet you. My name is [Name].
```

---

### 2. 介绍学校和专业

**基础模板**:
```
I'm a [year] student majoring in [major] at [university].
```

**替换词汇**:
- year: freshman (大一), sophomore (大二), junior (大三), senior (大四), final-year student (应届生)
- major: Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology
- university: 直接用学校名

**进阶表达**:
```
I'm currently pursuing my undergraduate degree in [major] at [university].
I'm an undergraduate student specializing in [major] at [university].
```

---

### 3. 说明考研准备

**基础模板**:
```
I'm preparing for the postgraduate entrance examination.
```

**进阶表达**:
```
I'm currently preparing for the national postgraduate entrance examination, hoping to pursue a master's degree in [field].

I'm in the process of preparing for the graduate school entrance exam, with plans to specialize in [field].
```

**说明目标**:
```
My goal is to pursue a master's/PhD degree in [field].
I hope to continue my studies in [field] at the graduate level.
```

---

### 4. 介绍研究兴趣

**基础模板**:
```
My research interest is [field].
I'm interested in [field].
```

**进阶表达**:
```
My research interests lie in [field], particularly in [specific area].

I'm particularly fascinated by [field], especially [specific aspect].

What really excites me is [field] and its applications in [domain].
```

**计算机专业常见研究方向**:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Machine Learning (ML) / Deep Learning (DL)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision (CV)
- Distributed Systems
- Operating Systems
- Computer Networks
- Database Systems
- Software Engineering
- Cybersecurity
- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

---

### 5. 说明学英语的原因

**基础模板**:
```
I'm learning English because I want to [reason].
```

**常见原因 + 地道表达**:

**阅读学术论文**:
```
✅ I want to read academic papers and stay updated with cutting-edge research.
✅ Reading research papers in English is essential for keeping up with the latest developments.
```

**国际交流**:
```
✅ I hope to communicate with international researchers and collaborate on projects.
✅ I believe English is crucial for engaging with the global research community.
```

**技术文档**:
```
✅ Most of the best technical documentation is in English.
✅ I want to access high-quality technical resources that are primarily in English.
```

**出国留学**:
```
✅ I'm considering studying abroad in the future.
✅ I have plans to pursue further education overseas.
```

**开源社区**:
```
✅ I want to contribute to open-source projects and engage with the global developer community.
✅ Participating in international tech communities requires strong English skills.
```

## ❌ 常见错误纠正

### 错误1: Chinglish（中式英语）

**❌ 错误**: I am study in XX University.  
**✅ 正确**: I study at XX University. / I'm a student at XX University.

**为什么错**: study 是动词，前面不能加 am

---

**❌ 错误**: I very like computer science.  
**✅ 正确**: I really like computer science. / I'm very interested in computer science.

**为什么错**: very 不能修饰动词 like

---

**❌ 错误**: My major is study computer.  
**✅ 正确**: My major is Computer Science. / I major in Computer Science.

**为什么错**: major 是名词，不能说"学习电脑"

---

### 错误2: 时态错误

**❌ 错误**: I prepare for the exam now.  
**✅ 正确**: I'm preparing for the exam. / I'm currently preparing for the exam.

**为什么错**: 描述正在进行的动作用现在进行时

---

**❌ 错误**: I learn English for three years.  
**✅ 正确**: I've been learning English for three years.

**为什么错**: 持续到现在的动作用现在完成进行时

---

### 错误3: 介词搭配错误

**❌ 错误**: I'm interested about AI.  
**✅ 正确**: I'm interested in AI.

**固定搭配**: be interested in (对...感兴趣)

---

**❌ 错误**: I study in Computer Science.  
**✅ 正确**: I study Computer Science. / I major in Computer Science.

**说明**: 
- study at [university]: 在某大学学习
- study [subject]: 学习某学科
- major in [subject]: 主修某专业

---

### 错误4: 词汇选择不当

**❌ 错误**: I'm learning English because I want to see papers.  
**✅ 正确**: I'm learning English because I want to read papers.

**说明**: "阅读论文"用 read，不是 see

---

**❌ 错误**: My hobby is computer.  
**✅ 正确**: I'm passionate about computers. / I enjoy working with computers.

**说明**: hobby 后面通常接活动，不是物品

## 💡 实用表达积累

### 礼貌表达

```
It's a pleasure to meet you. (很高兴见到你)
It's an honor to meet you. (很荣幸见到你 - 对方地位高时)
Nice to meet you. (见到你很高兴 - 日常)
I'd love to hear more about your work. (我很想了解更多您的工作)
Thank you for your time. (感谢您的时间)
```

### 表达热情

```
I'm passionate about... (我对...充满热情)
I'm fascinated by... (我对...着迷)
What really excites me is... (真正让我兴奋的是...)
I find it incredibly interesting that... (我发现...非常有趣)
```

### 表达目标

```
My goal is to... (我的目标是...)
I hope to... (我希望...)
I'm aiming to... (我的目标是...)
I aspire to... (我渴望... - 较正式)
```

## 🎓 写作练习

### 练习任务

现在轮到你了！请根据你的真实情况，写一段5-8句话的英文自我介绍。

**写作框架**:
1. 开头问候 + 姓名
2. 学校 + 专业 + 年级
3. 考研准备情况
4. 研究兴趣（具体一点）
5. 学英语的原因（1-2个理由）
6. 结尾礼貌用语

**写作建议**:
- ✅ 用简单句为主，避免复杂从句（清晰>复杂）
- ✅ 检查时态一致性
- ✅ 检查主谓一致
- ✅ 注意介词搭配
- ✅ 写完后大声读一遍，看是否流畅

---

## 📊 自我评估标准

完成写作后，对照以下标准自我评估：

### 内容完整性 (5分)
- [ ] 包含姓名
- [ ] 介绍学校和专业
- [ ] 提到考研准备
- [ ] 说明研究兴趣
- [ ] 解释学英语的原因

### 语法准确性 (3分)
- [ ] 时态正确
- [ ] 主谓一致
- [ ] 介词搭配正确

### 表达地道性 (2分)
- [ ] 避免明显的Chinglish
- [ ] 使用自然的英文表达

**总分 8-10分**: 🌟 优秀，表达清晰地道  
**总分 6-7分**: ✅ 良好，基本达意，有小问题  
**总分 4-5分**: 📖 中等，能理解但不够自然  
**总分 0-3分**: 💪 需要加强基础
