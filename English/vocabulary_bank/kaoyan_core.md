## Week 0-1: 基线测试词汇 + List 1

### Day 1 基线测试词汇

#### 1. arbitrary `/ˈɑːrbɪtreri/`
- **考研义**: adj. 任意的，武断的，专制的
- **词根**: arbitr(判断) + ary → 凭主观判断的
- **真题搭配**: arbitrary decision (武断的决定)
- **例句**: The choice of participants was not arbitrary.
- **反义词**: reasonable, justified

#### 2. prominent `/ˈprɑːmɪnənt/`
- **考研义**: adj. 突出的，显著的，著名的
- **词根**: pro-(向前) + min(突出) + ent → 向前突出的
- **真题搭配**: prominent feature (显著特征)
- **例句**: She played a prominent role in the project.
- **同义词**: notable, distinguished, conspicuous

#### 3. subordinate `/səˈbɔːrdɪnət/`
- **考研义**: adj. 次要的，下级的；n. 下属；v. 使从属于
- **词根**: sub-(在下) + ordin(顺序) + ate → 在下面的
- **真题搭配**: subordinate to (从属于)
- **例句**: All other issues are subordinate to this one.
- **反义词**: superior, dominant

#### 4. legitimate `/lɪˈdʒɪtɪmət/`
- **考研义**: adj. 合法的，正当的，合理的
- **词根**: leg(法律) + itim + ate → 符合法律的
- **真题搭配**: legitimate concern (合理的担忧)
- **例句**: That's a perfectly legitimate question.
- **派生**: legitimacy (n. 合法性)

#### 5. contemplate `/ˈkɑːntəmpleɪt/`
- **考研义**: v. 沉思，考虑，打算
- **词根**: con-(共同) + templ(庙) + ate → 在庙里共同思考
- **真题搭配**: contemplate doing sth (考虑做某事)
- **例句**: She contemplated leaving her job.
- **同义词**: consider, ponder, meditate

#### 6. dilemma `/dɪˈlemə/`
- **考研义**: n. 困境，进退两难
- **词根**: di-(二) + lemma(前提) → 两个前提都不好
- **真题搭配**: face a dilemma (面临困境)
- **例句**: The government faces a dilemma over education funding.
- **固定搭配**: be in a dilemma, pose a dilemma

#### 7. enthusiasm `/ɪnˈθuːziæzəm/`
- **考研义**: n. 热情，热忱
- **词根**: en-(进入) + thus(神) + iasm → 神灵附体的状态
- **真题搭配**: with enthusiasm (热情地)
- **例句**: She shows great enthusiasm for her work.
- **派生**: enthusiastic (adj. 热情的)

#### 8. perception `/pərˈsepʃn/`
- **考研义**: n. 感知，认知，看法
- **词根**: per-(完全) + cept(抓住) + ion → 完全抓住 → 感知
- **真题搭配**: public perception (公众看法)
- **例句**: Our perception of reality is often flawed.
- **动词**: perceive (v. 感知，察觉)

#### 9. implement `/ˈɪmplɪment/`
- **考研义**: v. 实施，执行；n. 工具，器具
- **词根**: im-(进入) + ple(填满) + ment → 填满 → 完成
- **真题搭配**: implement a policy (实施政策)
- **例句**: The company will implement the new system next month.
- **名词形式**: implementation (n. 实施)

#### 10. hypothesis `/haɪˈpɑːθəsɪs/`
- **考研义**: n. 假设，假说
- **词根**: hypo-(在下) + thesis(放置) → 放在下面的前提
- **真题搭配**: test a hypothesis (检验假设)
- **例句**: Scientists formulate hypotheses and test them.
- **复数**: hypotheses; 形容词: hypothetical
